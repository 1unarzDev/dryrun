# Local family usability pilot

Prepared October 17, 2026. No participants have been recruited or observed for this revision. This protocol measures
usability; it cannot establish reduced injuries, route safety, or clinical suitability. Do not record a real home,
medical note, name, address, contact number, voice, or face in shareable evidence.

## Consent and setup

Invite adults only after explicit recruitment authorization. Explain that this is a prototype, participation is
voluntary, they can skip any question or stop, and the exercise is fictional. Obtain explicit permission before
observing, timing or recording. Use fictional aliases, an example layout, and nonworking test phone numbers. A
participant may privately use their own data on their own phone, but do not copy it or upload its scan. Accessibility
accommodations are welcome and never require disclosure of a diagnosis. Include an unfamiliar household using an
accessibility accommodation if consenting testers are available.

## Uncoached tasks

1. From a fresh personal setup, skip address and scanning. Add one family member or keep You. Add a trusted contact.
2. Open the family card, select what it should share, find the contact, and preview the document. Target: useful
   preparation/card within about 90 seconds; measure rather than assume.
3. Using fictional locations, establish a near-home meeting point and a backup outside the neighborhood. Say where
   the family would reconnect if separated. Reopen the card and locate both text descriptions.
4. Explore the clearly marked example home. Practice home fire (target two to four minutes), identify the current
   affected room and one selected person's route or no-route explanation. Explain why an earlier route is no longer
   current. Try tornado and flood, locating the shelter/refuge or explaining the unsupported state.
5. Resolve one practical preparation through its editor. Reopen Prepare and explain what changed. Distinguish a
   recorded physical check from an untested rehearsal assumption.
6. Preview a full plan, share to a local destination or invoke Print without sending to others. Relaunch and find
   the saved information offline. Do not expect fresh routes, search, alerts, or arbitrary map tiles offline.

Stop a task after three minutes of being stuck. Record the failure and offer help only after stopping the timer.
Ask neutrally, “What did you expect here?” Do not teach the intended navigation while timing. End by asking the
participant to explain the meeting place and the exercise route/shelter in their own words. Do not test memory of
unreviewed clinical advice.

## Observe and retain minimally

Use `usability-results-template.csv` locally. Assign random participant codes, record app commit/build, OS/device,
locale, broad accommodation settings if voluntarily shared, task time, wrong turns, abandonment, assistance, and
whether the intended information was found. Notes describe interface behavior, never personal circumstances.
Timing begins when each task is read and ends when the participant identifies the required saved result. Mark
unattempted tasks as not tested, not passed. Separate observed facts from interpretation and proposed fixes.

Keep consent separately from anonymous task results, with an agreed deletion date. Do not add participant data to
Git. Review confusing highlights, obscured actions, translation issues, and misunderstood route states before
another session. Report sample size and limitations; never invent feedback, professional approval, or outcomes.
