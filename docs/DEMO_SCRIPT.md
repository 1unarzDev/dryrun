This is a recording script, not evidence a recording or pilot exists. Complete the native handoff first.
Keep the Example home / Exercise label visible. Use fictional/anonymized data only. No survival score or
prediction of remaining safe time. Do not publish or submit without the owner's authorization.

| Beat | Working journey to demonstrate | What to explain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create household → Finish later → add contact → family card | A useful preparation and portable card do not require a scan, account, location permission or medical equipment |
| 2 | Prepare → Your family / Your home → optional address | Family facts, optional location and geometry are distinct; example geometry is explicitly fictional |
| 3 | Plan → address/map → near and backup meetup | Two meeting purposes; Apple suggestions are ordinary planning resources; saved details feed the card/PDF |

| 6 | End action → record one real preparation | A saved contact/place/supply location closes its own gap; two medical subjects remain distinct. A physical drill has its own dated record. | 7 | Plan → selected-content preview → PDF share/Print | Critical details, contacts, meetups, home and separate neighborhood panel; unavailable map still leaves useful text |
| 8 | Relaunch offline; switch EN/ES/VI | Retained essentials remain local; live Apple services are not promised offline; translations/safety wording are drafts |

`-demoHousehold hh-oak -demoScenario home-fire -demoStep start`, or replace scenario with `flash-flood`/`tornado`.
Use `-demoLocale en|es|vi`, `-disableAnimations`, and `-demoStep decision|end` for stage shots.

are usability targets, not measured outcomes. Use USABILITY_PROTOCOL and results template with consenting testers;
no testers, reviews, partnerships, endorsements or injury-reduction outcomes have been invented.
