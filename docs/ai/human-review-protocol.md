1. Explain synthetic fixture status and obtain participation consent; allow stopping and deleting notes.
2. Give the infant/dog/powered-device fixture or two-adult fixture. Measure elapsed time and number of steps to reach three useful preparations, without coaching navigation.
3. Ask participants to explain “unknown,” a critical cap, a map candidate's unverified capability and a hypothetical scenario assumption in their own words.

5. Run one decision and ask which consequence came from their action versus a new external incident. After a delay, ask them to recall the next concrete preparation.
6. Optionally follow up on whether one real preparation was completed; record actual completion only with their agreement, without inferring emergency safety outcomes.
