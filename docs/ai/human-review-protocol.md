# Human and family evaluation — pending

No responder, clinician, accessibility specialist, family or competition judge has reviewed this implementation. Synthetic fixtures and model reviewers are not substitutes. Record consent, reviewer role/date, evidence reviewed, disagreements and resolutions before using “human-adjudicated.”

Preparedness reviewer packet: root implementation reference, rubric-v0.json, source registry and dates/jurisdiction, two demo households, action/resource explanations, stress traces and score arithmetic. Ask a qualified preparedness reviewer to check fire/tornado/flood distinctions, shelter activation claims, CDC water assumptions, safe combustion boundaries and critical omissions. Ask relevant manufacturer/clinical reviewers to check only documented device/care boundaries; the product must not diagnose or prescribe runtimes/medical requirements.

Accessibility packet: same tasks through no-LiDAR/manual and photo paths, VoiceOver/text equivalents, limited mobility, assistance arrangements, low-budget/no-car examples, unknown/provisional language, and practice grading with extra reading time. Check equal credit for equivalent suitable low-cost preparations and no score based on physical ability, speed or equipment price.

Small consent-based usability protocol (suggested 5–8 adult household coordinators; no real sensitive uploads until deployment fit is verified):

1. Explain synthetic fixture status and obtain participation consent; allow stopping and deleting notes.
2. Give the infant/dog/powered-device fixture or two-adult fixture. Measure elapsed time and number of steps to reach three useful preparations, without coaching navigation.
3. Ask participants to explain “unknown,” a critical cap, a map candidate's unverified capability and a hypothetical scenario assumption in their own words.
4. Ask them to correct an inventory quantity and dismiss/revisit one possible image concern; measure correction effort/errors.
5. Run one decision and ask which consequence came from their action versus a new external incident. After a delay, ask them to recall the next concrete preparation.
6. Optionally follow up on whether one real preparation was completed; record actual completion only with their agreement, without inferring emergency safety outcomes.

Report denominators, missing responses and household-level results. Do not invent adoption, reduced injury, endorsement or competition outcomes. Real household/photo validation, native privacy/retention and audience compliance remain release prerequisites.
