# Provisional preparedness rubric

`content/ai/rubric-v0.json` is locked policy version `dryrun-rubric/0.1-draft`. Guidance supports particular preparations; the weights/caps are DryRun product policy, not official safety thresholds. Source registry remains `content/sources/sources.json`; critical text, jurisdiction fit and clinical/manufacturer boundaries require human review.

Preparation domain budgets: protection/escape/shelter 25, essential supplies 25, communication/reunification 20, household-specific continuity 20, maintenance/currency 10. Criteria have fixed atomic predicates, source IDs, applicability and freshness rules. Each criterion earns the fraction of its predicates established true; assessed failures count toward coverage. Unknown/outside-scope predicates earn zero and remain in the denominator. N/A requires the published applicability rule and evidence. Only wholly inapplicable domains redistribute weight.

`C` is the domain-weighted preparation score; `P` is 25 times the sum of four eligible competency credits (0, explicitly anchored 0.5, or 1), with unknown competencies still in the denominator. `Overall = min(0.9*C + 0.1*P, applicable cap)`. Confirmed critical gaps cap at 59; unverified critical requirements cap at 79. Rounding is display-only. No assessed evidence shows “Not assessed,” never 100. All initial results remain provisional.

The app should show: overall, preparation subscore, practice status/coverage, evidence coverage, named cap reasons, and three useful next actions. It measures documented preparedness under a draft rubric, not survival probability, injury risk, certification or family rank. More equipment, scans, photo quality, income, speed or repeat drill counts do not create points.

`HouseholdAssessment` compiles existing local facts and inventory into accepted predicates. Missing equipment documentation remains unknown; only an explicit recorded absence can establish a known gap. Existing equipment/carer/pet needs share fixed parent budgets. `ScoreCompiler` rejects duplicate criteria, unanchored credit, unsupported predicate IDs and arbitrary N/A. Council outputs remain review proposals; never turn status labels directly into facts or numeric credit.

The latest eligible practice attempt governs, even when interrupted. `IntegratedAIState.beginPractice` resumes an unfinished attempt; pre-feedback action IDs must point to actual user commits. Guided/adaptive training is not itself a scored practice check. `practice-variants-v0.json` describes the provisional equivalent-check protocol; native must implement reviewed competency anchors and accessibility-equivalent input before showing practice as validated. Variant comparability and content review remain pending; no difficulty multipliers are used.

Development: the 60 synthetic cases were split 40 development / 20 locked holdout before live council prompt changes. Their hash and IDs are in `holdout-lock.json`. They are policy-authored arithmetic/predicate references, **not human-adjudicated** cases. Changing a holdout-informed policy requires retiring that holdout and creating fresh untouched cases. Live reviewer/council agreement is evaluated on three development cases, not used as clinical/safety validation.

`stress-envelopes-v0.json` freezes the initial scored robustness boundary. `HouseholdAssessment.assess(stressFindings:)` accepts a shared-route counterexample only when its complete source household digest and home fingerprint still match. It changes the existing `escape.independentAlternative` predicate once, retaining the fixed parent weight. Unverified capabilities, arbitrary-duration depletion and other unsupported stress findings remain advisory. Stress outcomes never decrement real inventory or complete tasks.

Practice chronology is host-validated across both action entry paths. During an incomplete check every user commit must be captured by submitPracticeAction in order; omitted generic commits invalidate eligibility instead of allowing a later response to earn credit. Four captured observations close the check; subsequent training cannot rewrite its original answers.
