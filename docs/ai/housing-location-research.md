# Housing, windows, and location evidence

Primary-source review: 2026-10-19. This implements the source/privacy boundaries in `DryRun_AI_Implementation_Reference.md` §§5, 8–9; these sources do not validate DryRun as an emergency safety system. Research only: no real household coordinates or images were submitted, and no live Gemini call was made.

## Operational requirements

| Topic | Evidence and consequence for DryRun |
| --- | --- |
| Apartment/shared exits | [USFA home fire escape plans](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/home-fire-escape-plans/index.html) directs high-rise residents to know exit stairs, close the apartment door behind them, use stairs rather than elevators unless directed by the fire department, and reach an outside meeting place. Model the unit, shared corridor, stairs, and actual final exterior as distinct graph elements. A unit door opening into an unmodeled common area cannot establish exterior egress. Keep building-specific posted procedures visible and their verification separate. |
| Multiple stories | USFA says include doors/windows and find two ways out of rooms. An upper-story window is not automatically a usable escape route, and a floor label does not establish grade. Require person-specific operability/escape evidence, real connector endpoints, obstruction/incident state, and assistance feasibility. Test a failed stair dependency rather than granting a second independent route through the same stair. |
| Manufactured/trailer home | [NWS tornado guidance](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tornado-during) explicitly excludes mobile homes, sheds, tents, and storage facilities as safe shelter. Pass dwelling type to Gemini; host shelter eligibility must reject manufactured-home interior rooms even if windowless. Establish an advance sturdy-building plan with access/transport uncertainty. The reference §8 prohibits suggesting a generic distant shelter drive during a tornado warning; retain that stricter product boundary. |
| Windows/tornadoes | NWS says a basement, safe room, or interior room away from windows; avoid large open rooms. A room with a detected window is unsuitable for the simple windowless interior-room rule even when that window is not an escape candidate. Detection is proposed evidence, not a verified escape opening or structural shelter certification. Require review of missing/occluded windows and preserve unknown geometry; do not infer no windows from no detections. |
| Flooding | [NWS flood guidance](https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during) says do not walk/swim/drive through floodwater and follow official instructions. Relative indoor floor elevation, mapped terrain elevation, floor/threshold elevation, and a floodwater level are different quantities. No one number establishes a safe building, road, or evacuation route. |
| Wildfire | [US Forest Service, Wildfire Risk to Communities](https://www.fs.usda.gov/managing-land/fire/wildfirerisk) explicitly says these data are **not locally calibrated or fine scale** and are best considered aggregated across a community rather than neighborhood or individual-home scale. Carry geographic scale and dataset vintage with the rating. Use community risk for preparation relevance, never individual-home certainty, active fire proximity, an evacuation order, or route clearance. |
| Hurricane | [NWS before-hurricane checklist](https://www.weather.gov/safety/hurricane-plan) directs users to their local government/emergency-management office to identify hurricane evacuation areas. [TxDOT hurricane preparation](https://www.txdot.gov/safety/severe-weather/hurricane-preparation.html) provides evacuation and contraflow maps and says lanes **may** be converted during evacuation. Store jurisdiction, official zone/route reference, issuance/retrieval/expiry and verification independently. A static route brochure is not an active order or confirmation that contraflow is operating. A generic MapKit route cannot acquire official evacuation status. |

## Elevation integration contract

[USGS 3DEP products/services](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services) describes terrain DEMs, resolutions, source-dependent accuracy, and freely available unrestricted 3DEP products. The [USGS Elevation Point Query Service OpenAPI](https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/docs), retrieved successfully, documents the `https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json` endpoint with x/y, spatial reference, `Feet`/`Meters`, and optional date. Its response describes value, raster ID and resolution. It does **not** document a surveyed finished-floor measurement or guarantee a vertical datum/accuracy field in each response. Do not invent either from a scalar response.

[NOAA NGS vertical datums](https://geodesy.noaa.gov/datums/vertical/) is the official reference-frame/datum resource and links datum conversions. Carry units, measurement type, horizontal reference, vertical datum (including explicit unknown), source dataset/version, acquisition/retrieval dates, resolution/uncertainty, and review status. Refuse a flood-level-versus-floor comparison when datums, measurement types, or required uncertainties are unknown/incompatible. A conversion needs an actual documented transformation; renaming the datum is insufficient. Keep site coordinates on the permitted native/official adapter path and send only the minimum normalized evidence needed after cloud opt-in. Exact home location is unnecessary for Gemini to explain an elevation uncertainty.

The FEMA NFHL and FloodSmart pages returned HTTP 403 to this Linux fetch; no successful live FEMA layer/API validation is claimed. Do not substitute fabricated flood-zone or base-flood-elevation results. Official jurisdiction links and manual reviewed evidence remain useful while layer integration is pending.

## Models for 2D and 3D checks

Two first-party buildingSMART repositories were inspected through their README/license and GitHub trees:

* [Certification-datasets](https://github.com/buildingSMART/Certification-datasets) has `Simple-Scene/Building-Architecture.ifc` in IFC 2x3, IFC4 and IFC4.3 directories. Its [license](https://github.com/buildingSMART/Certification-datasets/blob/main/LICENSE) is CC BY 4.0. The old `buildingSMART/Sample-Test-Files` URL redirects here. These are implementation/certification datasets, not verified emergency plans.
* [Community-Sample-Test-Files](https://github.com/buildingSMART-community/Community-Sample-Test-Files) includes `IFC 2.3.0.1 (IFC 2x3)/Duplex Apartment/Duplex_A_20110907.ifc`, a spaces model, preview images, and a per-model README. Its [root README](https://github.com/buildingSMART-community/Community-Sample-Test-Files/blob/main/README.md) states contributions use CC BY 4.0, warns the examples are not official buildingSMART examples, and says most fail its validation service. Check per-model author/README and preserve attribution before bundling assets; do not treat accompanying third-party product PDFs as automatically cleared by a root statement.

IFC provides a plausible common source for plan projection and 3D conversion, but these files have **not** been imported, validated, converted, or rendered here. No verified licensed manufactured-home example was found in this bounded review. An attractive external mesh without stable rooms/portals, window semantics, common-area boundaries, or route evidence cannot prove household logic.

For portable acceptance, prefer original deterministic DryRun `HomeModel` fixtures: apartment unit plus shared corridor and stairs/exterior, manufactured home with window-bearing rooms and advance external-shelter dependency, and two-story house with known elevations/stairs/windows. Feed the **same IDs and geometry** to the existing plan and dollhouse presentation/export paths. Assert floor transforms, opening positions, window counts, bounds, connector endpoints and route/shelter outcomes in both representations; export reviewable 2D and 3D artifacts. This is executable shared-geometry coverage, not a claim that RealityKit, RoomPlan detection, real scans, or device rendering passed. The external IFC models can be a separately attributed Mac import/visual-comparison exercise once an actual importer is available.

## Acceptance and privacy checks

1. Gemini gets anonymized dwelling type, relevant floors/window/connector evidence, household assistance needs, and normalized site-risk provenance/unknowns. Home labels, exact addresses and provider map listings stay out of generic context.
2. Window detection/review changes shelter evaluation and preparation guidance without creating verified operability or escape access. Manufactured-home protection is enforced by the host even if a model proposes a windowless room.
3. Apartment routes end at actual exterior nodes; corridor and stair failures propagate to rehearsal/stress tests. Upper-floor and mobility dependencies remain visible rather than being hidden by a successful path for another person.
4. Terrain values cannot change indoor floor transforms or clear flood routes. Wildfire community data cannot become live incident data. Static hurricane references cannot become current orders.
5. Stale or revoked location evidence remains visibly stale/unknown; refresh does not rerun unrelated private household/image inputs. Manual editing/offline plans remain usable.
6. Device/human review must evaluate small-screen output for one clear next action, a short household-specific reason, and material unknowns. Schema maximum lengths alone do not establish helpful depth. Preserve expandable evidence detail within the existing flows.
